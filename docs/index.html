Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 7, 2026

Welcome to Nothing Ever Happens. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data we collect or process in connection with your use of our mobile application (the "App") and related backend services.

This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the App.

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide a synchronized, multi-user todo-list experience, we collect and store certain information when you interact with our App:

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your email address, name, and profile picture URL. This is used strictly for authentication and to display your identity to other members of your shared household.
  • User-Generated Content: We collect and store the tasks, task descriptions, categories, completion statuses, and history logs that you create in the App. This data is synced to our secure databases to allow you and your household members to collaborate on tasks in real-time.
  • Device and Usage Information: We may collect diagnostic data, such as crash reports and error logs, to identify bugs and improve the stability of the App.

2. Third-Party Services

We use third-party infrastructure and services provided by Google (Firebase) to host our App and manage data safely. These services include:

  • Firebase Authentication: Used to manage user sign-in and secure accounts.
  • Cloud Firestore: A secure cloud database used to store and sync your tasks and logs in real-time.
  • Firebase Hosting: Used to serve our web application interface.

These third-party providers process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. You can read the Firebase Privacy Policy here: Firebase Privacy and Security Policies.

3. Offline Storage

To support offline functionality, the App caches task data locally on your device. This cached data is synced automatically with our cloud database once an internet connection is established. You can clear this local data at any time by logging out of the App or clearing the App cache in your device settings.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account details and user-generated tasks for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the services. If you wish to delete your account or delete all task data associated with your account, please contact us using the details below.

5. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information, and we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. We secure access to our databases using Firebase Security Rules, ensuring that only authenticated users belonging to your household can read or write your task data.

However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is not designed for or targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete this from our servers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.

8. Contact Us
