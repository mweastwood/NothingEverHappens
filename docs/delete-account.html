Under Google Play policies, Nothing Ever Happens (developed by mweastwood) provides transparent options for deleting your user account and all associated personal data.

⚠️ Action is Permanent: Once your account is deleted, your data cannot be recovered. Any active session in the app will be terminated immediately.

Alternative: In-App Account Deletion

You can also request the deletion of your account directly from within the mobile or desktop application:

  1. Open the Nothing Ever Happens app on your device.
  2. Ensure you are logged in to the account you wish to delete.
  3. Navigate to the Settings screen.
  4. Scroll down and select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the request when prompted. Your account and synced task data will be immediately and permanently removed.

What Data Is Deleted?

When you delete your account (either via the web form above or in-app), the following data is permanently purged from our Firebase Auth and Cloud Firestore servers:

  • Account Profile Data: Display name, email address, profile picture URL, and system User ID (UID).
  • User-Generated Content: All tasks, categories, task check-off dates, and history logs created by your account.
  • Recipes & Custom Settings: All personalized scheduling presets and customized configuration parameters.
  • Family Links & Invites: Any memberships in family groups or pending invitations sent to/from your email.
  • Device Tokens & Sessions: Active login tokens and credentials used to authorize updates.

What Data Is Kept / Retention Period

  • No User Data is Kept: We do not retain any of your personal identifiers or task lists once deletion is processed.
  • Backups: Encrypted backup snapshots of the Firebase databases are automatically rotated and completely overwritten/deleted within 30 days.
